Home About GRITLINE

Built by Mining Engineers. Driven by Sieve Precision.

Since 2009, GRITLINE Aggregates has transformed traditional stone crushing into a scientifically calibrated, transparent supply chain for India’s premier builders and highway contractors.

Integrated Quarry Infrastructure

3-Stage VSI Crushing & Pugmill Blending Operations

Unreliable aggregate suppliers buy from fragmented small crushers with worn jaw plates, resulting in flaky stones and high-silt sand that weaken structural concrete.

At GRITLINE, we control 84 hectares of high-specific-gravity Deccan basalt leases. Every ton passes through Swedish-engineered primary jaw crushers, secondary cone crushers, and tertiary Vertical Shaft Impactors (VSI) coupled with dual hydro-cyclone washing towers.

450 TPH Crushing Capacity 84 Hectares Approved Leases 3 Electronic 100-MT Weighbridges
17 Years of Growth

Our Quarry Evolution Timeline

How we scaled from a single 100-TPH basalt crusher to a multi-quarry regional aggregate leader.

2009 // Founding

Chakan Lease Pit-01 Commissioned

Mining Engineer Rajesh Deshmukh establishes GRITLINE with a single 100 TPH jaw-cone crushing line and 6 tipper trucks serving local industrial plots.

2014 // VSI & Wash Plant

First Hydro-Washed M-Sand Line

Pioneered Vertical Shaft Impactor shaping and hydro-cyclone silt extraction, enabling tier-1 RMC plants to transition 100% away from dredged river sand.

2019 // NABL Lab & Fleet

Automated Moisture Tare & 40 Tippers

Commissioned our on-site geotechnical testing lab and microwave moisture sensors so customers never pay for surface water weight in washed sand.

2026 // Industry Benchmark

4 Quarries & 68 GPS Hyva Tippers

Crossed 2.45 Million Metric Tons supplied across expressways, metro rail corridors, and commercial towers with 99.2% on-time slot compliance.

Core Principles

Our Mission & Non-Negotiable Quarry Values

Every dispatch slip we issue is backed by four operational commitments to your project engineers and purchase team.

Pillar 01

Weighbridge Honesty

Zero tolerance for volume-bulking tricks or short-loading. Every load is billed strictly on dry net metric tonnage with stamped digital slips.

Pillar 02

Sieve-Grade Precision

We change manganese crusher liners and wire-mesh screens on proactive schedules so particle gradation never drifts out of IS 383 envelopes.

Pillar 03

Clockwork Dispatch

Dedicated night-shift loading crews and owned tippers guarantee your concrete batching plant or site pour never stalls waiting for stone.

Pillar 04

Statutory Integrity

100% legal royalty passes, GST e-Way bills, pollution control compliance, and closed-loop water recycling for hassle-free project audits.
